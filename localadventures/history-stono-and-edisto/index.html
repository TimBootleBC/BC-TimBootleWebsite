Day trip

Charleston Historical Markers: the Stono and Edisto

Rantowles, Meggett, and Edisto Island, SC

Total driving: about 3 hr 10 min (Hanahan → stops → Hanahan; not counting time at stops)

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers along the Stono River, in Meggett, and on Edisto Island. It has 6 stops, ordered for a driving loop.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: Grave of Colonel William A. Washington historical marker

    About 21 min from Hanahan

  1. Grave of Colonel William A. Washington historical marker

    About 21 min from Hanahan

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Rantowles for Grave of Colonel William A. Washington. The state approved the marker in 1991. The inscription notes the year 1791. The official program groups this marker under S.C. in the Early Republic (1784-1815).

  2. The Stono Rebellion historical marker

    About 4 min from the previous stop

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Rantowles for The Stono Rebellion. The state approved the marker in 2006. The inscription notes years from 1739 to 1865. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  3. St. Paul's Parish Church historical marker

    About 16 min from the previous stop

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Ravenel for St. Paul's Parish Church. The state approved the marker in 2021. The inscription notes years from 1706 to 1812. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  4. St. Paul's Stono / St. Paul's Churchyard historical marker · Learn more

    About 25 min from the previous stop

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Meggett vicinity for St. Paul's Stono / St. Paul's Churchyard. The state approved the marker in 2002. The inscription notes years from 1652 to 1736. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  5. Plainsfield Plantation historical marker

    About 9 min from the previous stop

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Meggett for Plainsfield Plantation. The state approved the marker in 2011. The inscription notes years from 1663 to 1700. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  6. Edisto Island Baptist Church historical marker · Learn more

    About 42 min from the previous stop

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Edisto Island for Edisto Island Baptist Church. The state approved the marker in 2025. The inscription notes years from 1780 to 1860. The official program groups this marker under Antebellum (1816-1860).
