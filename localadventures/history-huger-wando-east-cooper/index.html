Berkeley Historical Markers: Huger, Wando, and East Cooper

Huger and Wando, SC · 15 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers around Huger, Wando, and East Cooper. It has 9 stops, ordered for a driving loop. The first stop is about 15 mins from Hanahan.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, official SCDAH marker map, and the Berkeley Outdoor Life historical markers map.

  • Start here: Quintyne Plantation historical marker
  1. Quintyne Plantation historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Charleston vicinity for Quintyne Plantation. The state approved the marker in 2019. The inscription notes years from 1679 to 1716. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  2. White House Plantation historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Goose Creek for White House Plantation. The state approved the marker in 2010. The inscription notes years from 1731 to 1941. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  3. Etiwan Island historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Daniel Island for Etiwan Island. The state approved the marker in 2024. The inscription notes years from 1609 to 1715. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  4. St. Thomas Church historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Huger for St. Thomas Church. The inscription notes years from 1706 to 1819. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  5. Brabant Plantation historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Huger for Brabant Plantation. The inscription notes years from 1732 to 1801. The official program groups this marker under Era of American Revolution (1764-1783).

  6. Pompion Hill Chapel historical marker · Learn more

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Huger for Pompion Hill Chapel. The inscription notes years from 1703 to 1763. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  7. New Hope Methodist Church historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Huger for New Hope Methodist Church. The state approved the marker in 2017. The inscription notes years from 1837 to 1910. The official program groups this marker under New South (1877-1913).

  8. Quenby Bridge historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Huger for Quenby Bridge. The inscription notes the year 1781. The official program groups this marker under Era of American Revolution (1764-1783).

  9. Silk Hope Plantation historical marker · Learn more

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Huger for Silk Hope Plantation. The inscription notes years from 1644 to 1709. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).
