Trip 79

Charleston Peninsula Walking Tour: Upper Peninsula and the East Side

Charleston, SC · 20 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

A walking tour of official South Carolina historical markers on the upper Charleston peninsula and East Side. It has 18 stops, ordered for a walking loop. The first stop is about 20 mins from Hanahan.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: British Evacuation of Charleston historical marker
  1. British Evacuation of Charleston historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Charleston for British Evacuation of Charleston. The state approved the marker in 2025. The inscription notes years from 1782 to 1783. The official program groups this marker under Era of American Revolution (1764-1783).
