Charleston Peninsula Walking Tour: King Street and the College

Charleston, SC · 20 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

A walking tour of official South Carolina historical markers around King Street, the College of Charleston, and the central peninsula. It has 18 stops, ordered for a walking loop. The first stop is about 20 mins from Hanahan.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: St. Philip's Church historical marker · Learn more
  1. St. Philip's Church historical marker · Learn more

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Charleston for St. Philip's Church. The state approved the marker in 1968. The inscription notes years from 1749 to 1824. The official program groups this marker under S.C. in the Early Republic (1784-1815).
