Trip 77

Charleston Peninsula Walking Tour: South of Broad and the Waterfront

Charleston, SC · 20 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

A walking tour of official South Carolina historical markers south of Broad Street and along the lower Charleston peninsula waterfront. It has 18 stops, ordered for a walking loop. The first stop is about 20 mins from Hanahan.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: Stevens-Lather House / Birthplace of Preservation historical marker
  1. Stevens-Lather House / Birthplace of Preservation historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Charleston for Stevens-Lather House / Birthplace of Preservation. The state approved the marker in 2020. The inscription notes years from 1843 to 1956. The official program groups this marker under WWI and Interwar Period (1913-1941).
