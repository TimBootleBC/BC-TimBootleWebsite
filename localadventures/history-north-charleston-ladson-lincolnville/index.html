Day trip

Charleston Historical Markers: North Charleston, Ladson, and Lincolnville

North Charleston, SC

Total driving: about 2 hr 15 min (Hanahan → stops → Hanahan; not counting time at stops)

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers in North Charleston, Ladson, and Lincolnville. It has 22 stops, ordered for a driving loop.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: French Botanical Garden historical marker

    About 5 min from Hanahan

  1. French Botanical Garden historical marker

    About 5 min from Hanahan

    A South Carolina state historical marker in North Charleston for French Botanical Garden. The state approved the marker in 2008. The inscription notes years from 1746 to 1803. The official program groups this marker under S.C. in the Early Republic (1784-1815).
