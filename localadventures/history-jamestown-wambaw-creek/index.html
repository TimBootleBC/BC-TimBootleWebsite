Berkeley Historical Markers: Jamestown and Wambaw Creek

Jamestown, SC · 35 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers around Jamestown and the Wambaw Creek area in eastern Berkeley County. It has 7 stops, ordered for a driving loop. The first stop is about 35 mins from Hanahan.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, official SCDAH marker map, and the Berkeley Outdoor Life historical markers map.

  • Start here: Skirmish at Wells's Plantation historical marker
  1. Skirmish at Wells's Plantation historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Huger vicinity for Skirmish at Wells's Plantation. The state approved the marker in 2025. The inscription notes the year 1781. The official program groups this marker under Era of American Revolution (1764-1783).

  2. Wambaw Plantation historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Shulerville vicinity for Wambaw Plantation. The state approved the marker in 2026. The inscription notes years from 1756 to 1782. The official program groups this marker under Era of American Revolution (1764-1783).

  3. Skirmish at Wambaw Bridge / Skirmish at Wambaw Creek historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Honey Hill vicinity for Skirmish at Wambaw Bridge / Skirmish at Wambaw Creek. The state approved the marker in 1997. The inscription notes the year 1782. The official program groups this marker under Era of American Revolution (1764-1783).

  4. Skirmish at Wambaw Bridge historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in McClellanville vicinity for Skirmish at Wambaw Bridge. The state approved the marker in 2025. The inscription notes the year 1782. The official program groups this marker under Era of American Revolution (1764-1783).

  5. Skirmish at Tidyman's Plantation historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in McClellanville vicinity for Skirmish at Tidyman's Plantation. The state approved the marker in 2025. The inscription notes the year 1782. The official program groups this marker under Era of American Revolution (1764-1783).

  6. Old Jamestown historical marker · Learn more

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Jamestown for Old Jamestown. The state approved the marker in 1985. The inscription notes years from 1705 to 1706. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  7. Battle of Lenud's Ferry historical marker · Learn more

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Jamestown for Battle of Lenud's Ferry. The state approved the marker in 1940. The inscription notes the year 1780. The official program groups this marker under Era of American Revolution (1764-1783).
