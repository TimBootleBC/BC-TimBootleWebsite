Day trip

Bushy Park / Foster Creek Blueway

Goose Creek, SC

Total driving: about 30 min (Hanahan → stops → Hanahan; not counting time at stops)

All local adventures

This is a paddling trip. You will need a canoe or kayak. Launch at Bushy Park Boat Landing in Goose Creek for the official Berkeley County Blueways route into Foster Creek, a good place to see wading birds. The paddle passes the Naval Weapons Station; official guidance is not to go ashore on Foster Creek.

The landing is listed by Berkeley County Parks and Recreation and as a public landing in the SCDNR coastal boat landings guide. Trail details and the shore-access warning are on the Bushy Park / Foster Creek Blueway page.
