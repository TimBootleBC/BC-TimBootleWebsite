Day trip

Dorchester Historical Markers: Summerville and Old Dorchester

Summerville, SC

Total driving: about 2 hr 25 min (Hanahan → stops → Hanahan; not counting time at stops)

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers in Summerville and at Old Dorchester. It has 17 stops, ordered for a driving loop.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: Archdale Hall historical marker

    About 17 min from Hanahan

  1. Archdale Hall historical marker

    About 17 min from Hanahan

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Summerville vicinity for Archdale Hall. The state approved the marker in 2002. The inscription notes years from 1681 to 1886. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).
