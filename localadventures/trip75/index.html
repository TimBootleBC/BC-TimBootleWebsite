Berkeley Historical Markers: St. Stephen, Pineville, and Cross

St. Stephen, Pineville, and Cross, SC · 35 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers in St. Stephen, Pineville, Cross, and nearby upper Berkeley County. It has 19 stops, ordered for a driving loop. The first stop is about 35 mins from Hanahan.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, official SCDAH marker map, and the Berkeley Outdoor Life historical markers map.

  • Start here: Rehoboth Methodist Church historical marker · Learn more
  1. Rehoboth Methodist Church historical marker · Learn more

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Macbeth for Rehoboth Methodist Church. The state approved the marker in 2004. The inscription notes years from 1805 to 1977. The official program groups this marker under Antebellum (1816-1860).
