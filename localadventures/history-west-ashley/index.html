Charleston Historical Markers: West Ashley

West Ashley, SC

Total driving: about 1 hr 35 min (Hanahan → stops → Hanahan; not counting time at stops)

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers in West Ashley. It has 9 stops, ordered for a driving loop.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: Drayton Hall / Drayton Family historical marker · Learn more

    About 20 min from Hanahan

  1. Drayton Hall / Drayton Family historical marker · Learn more

    About 20 min from Hanahan

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Charleston for Drayton Hall / Drayton Family. The state approved the marker in 1984. The inscription notes years from 1715 to 1822. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  2. St. Andrew's Parish Church and Skirmish at St. Andrew's Parish Church historical markers · Learn more

    About 8 min from the previous stop

    Several official South Carolina historical markers stand at this stop in Charleston: St. Andrew's Parish Church and Skirmish at St. Andrew's Parish Church. The state approved the marker in 2021. The inscription notes years from 1706 to 2004. The official program groups these markers under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).
