Day trip

Berkeley Historical Markers: Moncks Corner, Pinopolis, and the Cooper River

Moncks Corner, SC

Total driving: about 3 hr 15 min (Hanahan → stops → Hanahan; not counting time at stops)

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers in Moncks Corner, Pinopolis, and along the Cooper River. It has 17 stops, ordered for a driving loop.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, official SCDAH marker map, and the Berkeley Outdoor Life historical markers map.

  • Start here: Spring Grove Plantation historical marker

    About 21 min from Hanahan

  1. Spring Grove Plantation historical marker

    About 21 min from Hanahan

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Moncks Corner for Spring Grove Plantation. The state approved the marker in 2008. The inscription notes years from 1706 to 2003. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).
