Trip 72

Berkeley Historical Markers: Goose Creek, Hanahan, and Mount Holly

Goose Creek and Hanahan, SC · 10 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers around Goose Creek, Hanahan, and Mount Holly. It has 30 stops, ordered for a driving loop. The first stop is about 10 mins from Hanahan.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, official SCDAH marker map, and the Berkeley Outdoor Life historical markers map.

  • Start here: Bowen's Corner historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Hanahan for Bowen's Corner. The state approved the marker in 2008. The inscription notes years from 1680 to 1954. The official program groups this marker under Antebellum (1816-1860).
