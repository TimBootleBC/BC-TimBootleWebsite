Day trip

Dorchester Historical Markers: St. George, Harleyville, and Ridgeville

St. George, Harleyville, and Ridgeville, SC

Total driving: about 4 hr 5 min (Hanahan → stops → Hanahan; not counting time at stops)

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers in St. George, Harleyville, Ridgeville, and northern Dorchester County. It has 17 stops, ordered for a driving loop.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: Cypress Methodist Camp Ground historical marker · Learn more

    About 36 min from Hanahan

    About 36 min from Hanahan

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Ridgeville vicinity for Cypress Methodist Camp Ground. The state approved the marker in 2009. The inscription notes years from 1745 to 1978. The official program groups this marker under S.C. in the Early Republic (1784-1815).
