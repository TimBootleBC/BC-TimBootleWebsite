Trip 67

Childsbury Towne Heritage Preserve

Moncks Corner, SC · 55 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

A short trail on this S.C. Department of Natural Resources heritage preserve leads to an overlook of the upper Cooper River at the site of the early 1700s town of Childsbury and Strawberry Landing. Park where the pavement ends on Strawberry Chapel Road. The preserve is open from one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset. Neighboring Strawberry Chapel is privately owned and is not open to the public.

Trail and parking directions are on the SC Trails listing, managed by SCDNR. Heritage preserve hours are in the SCDNR managed lands regulations.
