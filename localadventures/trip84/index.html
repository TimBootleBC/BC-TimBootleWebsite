Trip 84

Charleston Historical Markers: Mount Pleasant and the Barrier Islands

Mount Pleasant, SC · 25 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers in Mount Pleasant, Sullivan's Island, and Isle of Palms. It has 14 stops, ordered for a driving loop. The first stop is about 25 mins from Hanahan.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: Maybank Green / Hobcaw Plantation historical marker · Learn more
  1. Maybank Green / Hobcaw Plantation historical marker · Learn more

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Mount Pleasant for Maybank Green / Hobcaw Plantation. The state approved the marker in 2007. The inscription notes years from 1660 to 1859. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  2. Jacob Bond I'on (1782-1859) historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Mount Pleasant for Jacob Bond I'on (1782-1859). The state approved the marker in 1998. The inscription notes years from 1782 to 1859. The official program groups this marker under S.C. in the Early Republic (1784-1815).
