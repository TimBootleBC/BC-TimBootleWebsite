Dungannon Plantation Heritage Preserve

Hollywood, SC · 30 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

Hike trails through bald cypress-tupelo swamp set aside for wood storks and other wading birds. Sign in at the entrance; the boardwalk is closed from February through September during nesting season.
