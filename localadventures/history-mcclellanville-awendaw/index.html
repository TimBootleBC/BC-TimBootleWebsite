Charleston Historical Markers: McClellanville and Awendaw

McClellanville and Awendaw, SC · 35 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers in McClellanville, Awendaw, and northern Charleston County. It has 5 stops, ordered for a driving loop. The first stop is about 35 mins from Hanahan.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: Old Wappetaw Church historical marker · Learn more
  1. Old Wappetaw Church historical marker · Learn more

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Awendaw for Old Wappetaw Church. The state approved the marker in 1998. The inscription notes years from 1700 to 1786. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  2. Archibald Rutledge Birthplace and Point Plantation historical markers

    Several official South Carolina historical markers stand at this stop in McClellanville: Archibald Rutledge Birthplace and Point Plantation. The state approved markers here in 2000 and 2008. The inscription notes years from 1705 to 1984. The official program groups these markers under WWII and Postwar (1941-present).

  3. McClellanville historical marker · Learn more

    A South Carolina state historical marker in McClellanville for McClellanville. The state approved the marker in 1995. The inscription notes years from 1827 to 1947. The official program groups this marker under Antebellum (1816-1860).

  4. St. James Santee Parish Church historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in McClellanville vicinity for St. James Santee Parish Church. The state approved the marker in 1996. The inscription notes years from 1706 to 1973. The official program groups this marker under Colonial (pre-contact to 1763).

  5. Thomas Pinckney 1750-1828 / St. James, Santee historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in McClellanville for Thomas Pinckney 1750-1828 / St. James, Santee. The state approved the marker in 1989. The inscription notes years from 1787 to 1812. The official program groups this marker under S.C. in the Early Republic (1784-1815).
