St. Simons Island Lighthouse & Fort Frederica

St. Simons Island, GA

All 52 local adventures

Climb the 1872 lighthouse, walk under live oaks at Christ Church, and tour General Oglethorpe’s 1736 British fort ruins.

  • Christ Church Frederica, Frederica Road, St. Simons Island, GA
