Day trip

Charleston Historical Markers: James Island and Folly Beach

James Island and Folly Beach, SC

Total driving: about 2 hr 50 min (Hanahan → stops → Hanahan; not counting time at stops)

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers on James Island, Folly Beach, and nearby creeks. It has 22 stops, ordered for a driving loop.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: Battery Haig historical marker

    About 20 min from Hanahan

  1. Battery Haig historical marker

    About 20 min from Hanahan

    A South Carolina state historical marker in James Island for Battery Haig. The state approved the marker in 2006. The inscription notes years from 1863 to 1865. The official program groups this marker under Civil War (1861-1865).
