Trip 82

Charleston Historical Markers: Johns Island and Wadmalaw

Johns Island and Wadmalaw Island, SC · 20 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

A driving tour of official South Carolina historical markers on Johns Island and Wadmalaw Island. It has 12 stops, ordered for a driving loop. The first stop is about 20 mins from Hanahan.

Stops follow official South Carolina historical markers approved by the S.C. Department of Archives and History. Short notes below are written in our own words; read the inscription on the marker itself. See the South Carolina Historical Marker Program, and the official SCDAH marker map.

  • Start here: St. Mark's Union Methodist Episcopal Church historical marker
  1. St. Mark's Union Methodist Episcopal Church historical marker

    A South Carolina state historical marker in Johns Island vicinity for St. Mark's Union Methodist Episcopal Church. The state approved the marker in 2023. The inscription notes years from 1885 to 1969. The official program groups this marker under New South (1877-1913).
