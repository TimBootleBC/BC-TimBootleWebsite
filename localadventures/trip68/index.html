Trip 68

Goose Creek Reservoir Blueway

Hanahan, SC · 10 mins · from Hanahan, SC

All local adventures

This is a paddling trip. You will need a canoe or kayak. Launch at Bettis Boat Landing and explore the official Berkeley County Blueways route on Goose Creek Reservoir, a local lake with coves, cypress, and wading birds. The Blueways also list a north access at Venice Avenue Park in Hanahan.

The landing is listed by the City of Hanahan. Trail details are on the Goose Creek Reservoir South Blueway and North Blueway pages from Berkeley Outdoor Life, a program of the Berkeley Soil and Water Conservation District and Berkeley County.

  • Bettis Boat Landing — 3000 Railroad Avenue, Hanahan, SC 29410
  • Venice Avenue Park, Venice Avenue, Hanahan, SC 29410
