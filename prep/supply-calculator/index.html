3- and 14-day supply calculator

Last reviewed: 10 November 2026

A 3- and 14-day household supply calculator for a family of four, with adjustable adult and child counts and optional small, medium, and large pets, using 1 gallon of water and 2,000 calories per person per day.

This is general household information, not official emergency instructions. In an emergency, follow local officials, the National Weather Service, and call 911 if life is at risk.

This calculator uses the same planning rates as the rest of this section: 1 gallon of water per person per day from Ready.gov and the Red Cross, and a planning assumption of 2,000 calories per person per day, the general figure on U.S. Nutrition Facts labels (FDA). It is not a personal prescription. Needs vary by age and activity; see the USDA ranges on the food page. In very hot weather, Ready.gov notes that water needs can double, so treat the gallon as a floor.

The form defaults to a family of four (two adults and two children). You can change those numbers. Pets are optional and can be entered as small, medium, or large. Pet food is not given a calorie figure on this site, so it is not totaled here — pack several days of the food your animals already eat, as described on the pets page.

Other kit items such as a flashlight or headlamp for each person, or a power bank for each person who relies on a phone, are per-person counts rather than per-person-per-day rates. They are on the supply checklist and are not multiplied across 3 and 14 days here.
