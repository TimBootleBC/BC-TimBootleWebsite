Preparedness

Preparedness books

Last reviewed: 10 November 2026

A short, curated list of well-regarded books on household preparedness, emergency medicine, food storage, and outdoor skills, with plain, non-affiliate Amazon links.

This is general household information, not official emergency instructions. In an emergency, follow local officials, the National Weather Service, and call 911 if life is at risk.

This is a short reading list, not a shop, and a link is not an endorsement of any book, author, or seller. The links are ordinary Amazon product pages, not affiliate links. This site does not earn a commission if you buy a book. Editions and stock change, so confirm the title and edition on the product page before you order. These books are information only; they are not a substitute for official guidance, training, or professional medical care. For household supplies, see where to buy supplies and the supply checklist.

Getting started

  • The Prepper's Blueprint by Tess Pennington (2014). A step-by-step household plan that starts with short-term events such as storms and outages, then builds toward longer disruptions.
  • Handbook to Practical Disaster Preparedness for the Family, 3rd Edition by Arthur T. Bradley (2012). A family-focused handbook covering the usual household needs — water, food, shelter, power, medical care, and communications — with planning worksheets.
  • The Unthinkable (Revised and Updated) by Amanda Ripley (2024). A look at how people actually behave in disasters, and what helps families make better decisions under stress.

Medical

  • Where There Is No Doctor by David Werner, Carol Thuman, and Jane Maxwell (Hesperian revised edition, 2025 printing). A widely used plain-language village health handbook for prevention, common illnesses, and care when a clinic is far away.
  • The Survival Medicine Handbook by Joseph Alton, MD, and Amy Alton, APRN (4th edition, 2021). A comprehensive reference for first aid and medical care when professional help is delayed or unavailable.
  • Herbal Medic: A Green Beret's Guide to Emergency Medical Preparedness and Natural First Aid by Sam Coffman (2021). A handbook on first-aid essentials and herbal medicine for when professional medical care is unavailable; it is information only, not medical advice.

Food and water

  • Emergency Food Storage & Survival Handbook by Peggy Layton (2002). Practical advice for storing food and water, rotating what you eat, and building affordable household reserves.
  • Ball Complete Book of Home Preserving by Judi Kingry, Lauren Devine, and Sarah Page (2024 updated edition). The standard home-canning reference, with safety methods and recipes for putting up food at home.

Skills

  • SAS Survival Handbook, Third Edition by John 'Lofty' Wiseman (2014). A classic, illustrated field guide to wilderness and urban survival, including shelter, water, food, first aid, and navigation.
  • Bushcraft 101 by Dave Canterbury (2014). A practical introduction to wilderness skills such as choosing a kit, fire, shelter, tools, and using what is around you.
